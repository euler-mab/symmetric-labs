§ 01 · About

Theory translated into production infrastructure.

Symmetric Labs is the consulting practice of the former Chief Executive of Euler Labs — the team behind one of the most widely-used lending protocols in decentralised finance.

Before Euler, a decade of academic research across dynamical systems, evolutionary theory, bioinformatics and applied machine learning. DPhil from the University of Oxford in mathematical biology; postdoctoral work at Oxford and UCL.

The practice is deliberately small. Engagements are led personally.
